# 17. The three-week play (stage F8): a guide

Written 2026-11-15. Nothing more is built until you have played. Open the app, choose Goblin mode, and play one war the way you would play any game on your phone: a few minutes at a time, whenever you like. The world moves by itself on the hour, so there is no wrong way to be away.

## What to do

1. **Play for three real weeks** (about 21 days, or until you stop wanting to). A war ends in roughly two to three weeks; if you win it, take the next region and keep going.
2. **Keep the Digest as your door.** Open the game, read the headline and the few lines, do one thing, close it. That is the two-minute door (pillar 9).
3. **When something is wrong, bored or confusing, copy the Ledger** (Ledger, then Copy) and paste it to me with one line saying what you were trying to do. The Ledger is the war's own record, so I can replay it.
4. **Say which of these you used**, once a week, in a sentence: Scout, Taunt, Insult, Parley, Duel, a foothold run, Fortify, a hired band, a bridge, Guard, raiding a convoy, seeking the dwarves, a quiz charge, letting a prisoner go. The ones you never touched tell me what to cut.

## Known rough edges (so you do not have to report them)

- The bold style wins every war, quickly; the careful style wins most. Expect the rivals to feel soft in some places.
- A war's difficulty depends on where it starts (rock towns around Paterson give a buffer; Newark is harder).
- Ledger sentences are my placeholders; many read stiffly.
- The map's township names are hidden where you know nothing yet: that is the fog, not a bug.
- Saves are replaced by a new version of the game; a new version may start you a fresh war.

## What happens after

When you say how the three weeks went, I fix what is broken and tune what is wrong, then write the plan for the shared mode (stage F9: friends, a small server) if you want it. The words, names and jokes are yours whenever you are ready; none of them blocks play.
