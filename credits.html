3D buildings

Building heights come from NYC Open Data building footprints. The shapes of about 190 of the tallest towers (their setbacks, crowns and spires) are cut from the NYC 3D Model by Community District, published by the NYC Department of City Planning from the 2014 aerial survey of the NYC Department of Information Technology and Telecommunications. The towers finished since then (One Vanderbilt, Central Park Tower, 111 W 57th, 432 Park) are built from OpenStreetMap building parts, as republished by Overture Maps: © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL.
